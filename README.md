Mailtea provides an agent‑native email platform that combines transactional and marketing email capabilities with AI‑driven agent tools. Users can design visual emails, automate campaigns, manage audiences, and integrate with popular AI agents like Claude, Cursor, and Replit. The service offers a REST API, SMTP, inbound webhooks, and a CLI, plus a studio for collaborative content creation. It targets developers and teams seeking programmable email workflows enhanced by AI.

**Areas:** Email · AI · Platform · DeveloperTools · Marketing

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Mailtea: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
